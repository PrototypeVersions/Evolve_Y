01 / The foundation

Great technology.

Evolve Y is a proposed network of useful AI services, local expertise and shared infrastructure. Its business earns money by solving real problems. Its rules give participants a meaningful claim on the benefits.

03 / A horizon of possibility

Three futures.
One useful direction.

No one knows how quickly AI will transform the economy. The proposal should remain valuable across very different paths. Explore the possibilities below.

Uneven progress

AI gets better, adoption stays difficult.

Small organizations need help turning capability into reliable daily work. Sell a service that saves time after review, training and integration costs.

Local support + measured outcomes

Keep the business useful at today’s capabilities. Expand only when customers renew and quality holds.

Scenarios are design tests, not predictions. Universal high income would require durable funding, institutions and an adequate supply of real goods and services.

04 / Begin close to home

Big ambition.
Real-world beginnings.

Start in Dallas–Fort Worth with a shared operations desk for small property and maintenance organizations. Coordinate requests, documents, quotes, approvals and completion—then measure the difference.

First proposed application / MREO

One project. Everyone on the same page.

A resident reports a repair. A coordinator prepares the work. A professional delivers it. The authorized person approves costs. A portable record shows what happened and who is responsible.

Walk through a project ↗
  • A clear buyerSmall property operators pay for less administrative work and fewer missed steps.
  • A real test10–20 paying organizations, a 90-day pilot, and published quality and cost measures.
  • A larger purposeUse the same coordination foundation for learning, creator rights and community projects.
